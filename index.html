Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to Common Questions

Everything you need to know about Hkode security, school applications, and account recovery.

How does Hkode assist schools and educational institutions?
Hkode provides an uncompromised, zero-cloud encryption layer for teachers, professors, counselors, and students. It ensures exam questions, grading rubrics, counseling notes, and student records remain completely confidential, even when transmitted across public messaging apps, SMS, or unencrypted email.
Are my messages or secret keys stored on your servers?
No. Hkode operates on a 100% offline, zero-knowledge architecture. There is no central server, no remote database, and no cloud backup. All cryptography executes strictly inside your device’s local memory, making data breaches impossible.
What happens if I forget my 4-digit passcode?
When setting up a passcode, you mandatorily configure two personal security questions. If you ever forget your passcode, simply tap "Forgot Passcode?" on the lock screen, answer your two questions, and you will instantly unlock the app and be prompted to set a new passcode.
Can I use Hkode without Wi-Fi or cellular data?
Yes. Every feature of Hkode—including encryption, decryption, password vault access, and passcode verification—works completely offline without requiring any internet connection.
How does the recipient decrypt a message I send them?
The recipient simply opens Hkode, navigates to the Decrypt tab, pastes the ciphertext, and enters the pre-shared secret passphrase (or uses the Hkode default key). Decryption completes in milliseconds with full tamper-detection.
